---

## Context

As engineering organizations grow, knowledge becomes distributed across people, documentation, and systems.

## Observation

Many teams rely on informal knowledge sharing through meetings or messaging platforms.

This approach works for small teams but breaks down as systems grow more complex.

## Example

Large engineering environments require documentation systems that capture architectural decisions, operational workflows, and system behavior.

Without structured knowledge systems, teams repeatedly rediscover information that already exists.

## Takeaway

Knowledge systems should be designed intentionally to support engineering teams as products scale.
