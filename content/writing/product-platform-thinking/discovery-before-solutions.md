Many product efforts fail because teams jump to solutions before fully understanding the underlying problem.

## The tendency to solution quickly

Engineering teams are naturally inclined to build solutions. However, without careful discovery, teams risk solving the wrong problem.

## Why discovery matters

Product discovery helps teams understand:

- user workflows
- operational constraints
- existing system limitations

## Example from enterprise operations

In many SAP environments, engineers perform repetitive monitoring checks. At first glance, the solution appears to be automation scripts.

However, the deeper issue is often a lack of centralized operational visibility.

## Takeaway

Effective product development begins with understanding the problem space before committing to a solution.
