## Context

Many technology organizations provide operational services before building products.

## Observation

Services are typically customized for individual clients, while products aim to deliver repeatable solutions across many customers.

## Example

Enterprise operations teams often perform manual tasks such as monitoring, incident response, or infrastructure maintenance.

Over time, recurring operational tasks may be automated and packaged into software systems.

This transformation represents a shift from service delivery to product design.

## Takeaway

Product thinking often begins by identifying repeatable patterns within service workflows.
