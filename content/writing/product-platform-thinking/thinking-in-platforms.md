## Context

Platforms emerge when systems are designed to support multiple users, workflows, and services.

## Observation

Many internal tools begin as small utilities that solve specific problems for individual teams.

As these tools grow in capability, they often become shared platforms.

## Example

Developer portals, CI systems, and internal automation frameworks frequently begin as small team tools before evolving into organization wide platforms.

## Takeaway

Platform thinking requires designing systems that support extensibility, reuse, and multiple stakeholders.
