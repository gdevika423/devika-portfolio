This section contains essays and working notes on product thinking, platform design, developer experience, and knowledge systems.

Many of these ideas emerge while working on product discovery, documentation systems, and automation platforms.

Rather than focusing on specific project outcomes, these writings explore broader themes such as:

- how services evolve into products
- how platforms emerge from operational workflows
- how developer experience is shaped by knowledge systems
- how automation changes enterprise operations

Some pieces are structured essays, while others are short working notes that capture ideas still evolving.
