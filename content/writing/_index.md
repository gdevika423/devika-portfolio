- [Thinking in Platforms](/writing/product-platform-thinking/thinking-in-platforms/) - Why successful engineering organizations often evolve from tools to platforms.

- [Operations as Products](/writing/systems-operations/operations-as-products/) - How operational workflows can evolve into structured software products.
