## Context

Operations teams often manage complex systems through manual workflows.

## Observation

Many operational tasks follow predictable patterns that make them strong candidates for automation.

## Example

Monitoring systems, incident management workflows, and infrastructure maintenance processes can often be converted into structured software platforms.

## Takeaway

Viewing operations through a product lens can reveal opportunities to design scalable systems rather than relying on manual workflows.
