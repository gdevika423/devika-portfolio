## Context

Enterprise systems often involve large operational workloads performed by engineers.

## Observation

Many operational tasks such as monitoring, log analysis, and incident detection follow predictable workflows.

## Example

Automation systems can monitor signals across infrastructure environments and alert engineers when intervention is required.

This reduces manual monitoring effort and improves operational visibility.

## Takeaway

Automation becomes valuable when it reduces repetitive operational tasks while improving system reliability.
