## Discovery context

Nordia delivers SAP BASIS services across enterprise environments. Monitoring is continuous, manual, and dependent on specialist engineers.

The key discovery question was:

**Can SAP monitoring evolve from a manual service workflow into a scalable product capability?**

## Key insight

| Observation | Insight |
|---|---|
| Engineers repeatedly review system signals | Monitoring logic can be automated |
| Failures follow recognizable patterns | Rule-based detection is feasible |
| The same checks recur across environments | Monitoring capability is reusable across customers |

## Product hypothesis

A centralized monitoring engine could continuously evaluate operational signals and detect issues automatically, shifting engineers from manual monitoring to incident response.
