## Context

Enterprise SAP operations involve a large number of repetitive BASIS activities such as user administration, transport handling, monitoring, job checks, and log analysis.

These workflows are necessary, but they often depend on manual execution, fragmented scripts, and repeated operational effort.

## Problem

Manual BASIS operations create three recurring issues:

- high operational workload
- risk of human error
- slower incident detection and resolution

As operational complexity grows, service teams need better ways to standardize and scale repeatable work.

## Product hypothesis

If repetitive SAP BASIS tasks are converted into reusable automation services, teams can reduce manual effort, improve reliability, and build a foundation for a centralized operations platform.

## Discovery approach

The discovery phase focused on:

- understanding BASIS operational workflows
- identifying repetitive and high-friction activities
- mapping automation opportunities across domains
- exploring where AI-assisted analysis could add value

## Opportunity landscape

Initial automation opportunities were identified across:

- user administration
- transport management
- system monitoring
- job monitoring
- interface monitoring
- database management
- security and auditing
- cloud BASIS operations

## Product vision

The long-term vision is a platform that combines:

- reusable automation microservices
- workflow orchestration
- operational visibility
- AI-assisted analysis and recommendations

This shifts the model from isolated scripts to a more scalable SAP operations automation platform.

## Why this matters

This work sits at the intersection of documentation systems, enterprise operations, automation design, and platform product thinking.

It reflects how structured knowledge and systems design can directly shape product strategy.
