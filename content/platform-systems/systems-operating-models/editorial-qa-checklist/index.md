## Editorial QA Checklist

This checklist is designed to help documentation teams ensure that every page meets the necessary standards for technical accuracy, voice consistency, and overclaim risk before publication. It should be completed by documentation managers or senior writers in under five minutes per page.

### Technical Accuracy

- **Verification Requirement**: Ensure that all technical details have been verified by someone with direct product knowledge. The drafting writer may not have the complete context or latest updates.
- **Cross-Check with Product**: Confirm that the information aligns with the current state of the product. This includes features, functionalities, and any limitations.

### Voice and Tone Consistency

- **House Style Adherence**: Review the content to confirm it matches the site’s established voice and tone. The writing should be:
  - Direct
  - Evidence-led
  - Free from unsupported superlatives
- **Examples Needed**: Include specific examples of phrases or terms that exemplify the house style for reference.

### Claims Verification

- **Built vs. Planned**: Scrutinize every claim indicating that something is 'built' or 'live.' Verify these statements against the actual product as it stands, rather than what is planned for future releases.
- **Documentation of Claims**: Maintain a record of where each claim is verified. This could be a simple checklist or a reference to the relevant documentation.

### Final Review

- **Checklist Completion**: Ensure that all items on this checklist are addressed before publication. This includes:
  - Technical accuracy confirmed
  - Voice and tone consistent
  - Claims verified against the live product
- **Time Efficiency**: Aim to complete this checklist in under five minutes per page to maintain productivity while ensuring quality.

By following this checklist, you can help ensure that the documentation is accurate, consistent, and reliable, ultimately enhancing the user experience.
